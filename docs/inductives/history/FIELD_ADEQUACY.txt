Exact field adequacy: guard-free formation comparison
====================================================

Target and current consumer
---------------------------
Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Injectivity.lean, IsDefEqU.fieldType_inv_stratified
(currently beginning at line 47), compares two successful computations of the
same registered field. It assumes typed majors, equality of majors and their
family types, and the two ORIGINAL field formations at their respective
literal sorts and stratification bounds. It concludes field equality at the
first sort and equivalence of the two original sort levels. It does not assume
that either primitive field projection is permitted.

UniqueTyping.lean, IsDefEq.uniq, projection case (around line 70), currently
consumes this target. That particular consumer has actual projection typings,
and therefore their elimination guards. The public field theorem is broader.

AnchoredOriginalWorldBoundedCallBank.lean, WorldBoundedReplayAt.assigned,
compares assigned types of originals with the SAME displayed expression.
Using two primitive projections as that common expression requires genuine
projDF originals. This construction cannot cover the public field target.

Missing guard
-------------
The primitive rule requires

  (info.resultLevel.inst levels).IsNeverZero OR fieldLevel ≈ zero.

This is not implied by formation of the computed field type. Consider the
legal proposition-valued declaration shape

  S : Prop
  S.mk : (X : Type) -> S.

The major S.mk Prop inhabits S. Field zero has computed type Type (Sort 1),
which itself is well typed at Sort 2. The result level is zero, and the field
formation level is two. Neither disjunct of the elimination guard holds.
Taking identical levels, parameters and majors on both sides satisfies the
comparison hypotheses without making this data projection available.

This is a counterexample to deriving the projection guard, NOT a counterexample
to fieldType_inv_stratified: in this example the field types are identical.

Smallest remaining bridge
------------------------
A guard-free, formation-directed field/telescope congruence lemma should take:

* the actual registered constructor telescope and successful two fieldType
  computations;
* equivalent universe instantiations and corresponding parameter equalities,
  obtained from rigid-family equality, rather than assumed at the final target;
* actual equality and typing of the two majors;
* actual formations of the two resulting field expressions.

It should produce a TypeConversion between those field expressions. Original
assigned-sort compatibility must additionally identify their original literal
sort levels. Homogeneous readback then yields equality at fieldLevel1; retain
the original stratified witnesses rather than retype the second field at the
first literal sort with unchanged height.

The operative finite traversal must follow dependencies actually used by the
selected typed field. ProjectionData.lean, instantiateProjectionFields and
fieldType, substitute prior projection SYNTAX without checking its typing.
InstForallsC in ProjectionLemmas.lean requires every prefix argument typed and
is consequently too strong for this purpose. Even InstForalls.defeq requests
argument equalities for vacuous positions. The constructor-major proof near
ProjectionLemmas.lean:1000 can replace unused slots with genuine constructor
arguments; an arbitrary typed major does not supply those arguments.

Premises and circularities to avoid
----------------------------------
Do not add the elimination guard to the public target, assert a total typed
prior-projection prefix, or manufacture inhabitants for unused field domains.
Do not use UniqueTyping, fieldType_inv_stratified, or the existing high
InstForalls.defeq proof as independent foundation facts: the latter invokes
the currently admitted Pi inversion and uniqueness-based alignment helpers.
Do not infer field congruence from raw substitution equality alone. Do not
replace the missing formation-directed compiler by an assumed semantic answer.

All-budget banks provide powerful conditional interpretation/comparison
interfaces; the above identifies a missing constructive reduction to them,
not a proof that the field target cannot follow from the completed metatheory.

Alternative UniqueTyping endpoint
--------------------------------
The exposed IsDefEq.uniq statement does not request stratification bounds.
An independently proved assigned-type compatibility theorem for two typings
of the same term would bypass its current stratified proof entirely: extract
the two typings of the shared middle term e2 from the strong equality
derivations, compare their assigned types, and read back a homogeneous sort
equality. This leaves the exact public field theorem as a separate goal.
Concretely, WorldBoundedReplayAt.assigned can be requested at the empty code
profile: BoundedParameterReply.path (AnchoredOriginalBoundedGeneratedReply.lean,
around line 148) is an unconditional TypeConversion, not a capability that
requires a nonempty profile. Use identity displays and a genuinely constructed
initial frame for the two typings of e2. AnchoredInversionReadback's
TypeConversion.defeqOfSortUniqueness supplies the homogeneous readback once
original sort uniqueness has been derived independently from the banks.
The global assigned-C construction used for this bypass must itself be proved
without the old UniqueTyping/field theorem; otherwise the rewrite only hides
the existing dependency cycle.
