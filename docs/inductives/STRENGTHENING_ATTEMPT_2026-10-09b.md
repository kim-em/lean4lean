## 2. Designer's analysis of the recursion (why every measure fails so far)

Write a certificate calculus with guard nesting: `conv ⊃ red ⊃ step ⊃ ty ⊃ conv`. The
transitivity proof is a strip lemma whose diamonds re-express guards:

* a K/alignment guard `g : x ~ y` against an argument step `x → x'` composes
  `x' ← x`, `g` (frozen checks fix exactly this and nothing else: review 5);
* certified substitution at `ty_app`: the induction hypothesis returns `CTy f[σ] S` with
  `d : S ~ F[σ]`, while the exposure `F[σ] →* Π A[σ] B[σ]` starts at `F[σ]`; the argument
  guard of the substituted node is `A'[σ] ~ A''`, a composition of the substituted guard
  with a reduct comparison, both *outputs*;
* eta against a reduction of the function re-synthesises the domain;
* normal-equality transport at proof-irrelevance leaves composes `S ~ p[n] ~ p'[n] ~ p'[n']
  ~ S'`.

Depth stratification by guard nesting fails because proving transitivity for depth-`d`
joins builds a K-step whose guard is a depth-`d` zigzag, i.e. a depth-`(d+1)` step, so the
later tiles of the same strip need the diamond at depth `d+1`.

Regress argument for the counterexample side: the only rule that mentions the removed
variable `q` at the root is proof irrelevance `q ≡ t : Q↑` (when `Q : Prop`); every other
`q`-dependence enters through the typing of a `q`-free term at a type convertible to `Q↑`
only above, i.e. through a type-level instance of the same problem. So any counterexample
must contain a self-supporting cycle of `Q`-dependent conversions with that single base, or
a `q`-free term typable at `Q↑` above and not at `Q` below.

### B. Term model: integrate `TermEq`, then typing strengthening under uninhabitedness

Integrate `TermEq` and its theorems (`Strengthening/TermModel.lean`). Then the stepping
stone the regress argument suggests: `UninhabitedTypingFront`: if no term of type `Q`
exists in `Γ`, every `q`-free term typable in `Q :: Γ` is typable in `Γ` (at some type), by
structural induction on the term with the inversion lemmas (`app_inv`, `lam_inv`, ...) and
head inversion; identify the failing case (expected: exposing a lifted function type to a
`Π` in `Γ` from its exposure in `Q :: Γ`) and state the needed sub-lemma exactly; check
whether `UninhabitedTypingFront` gives `Cancel` (expected: it is the special case `a = b`,
and the general case needs the join repair as well).

### C. Counterexample hunt: the typing gap

Integrate Astra's ledger (`Strengthening/Candidates.lean`: `UninhabitedCancel ⇔ Cancel`,
K-alignment facts, quotient source inhabitant, eta/definition/unit/structure-eta above and
below, failure of the two invariants). Then hunt in the one gap left open by the ledger:
a `q`-free term `t` with `Q :: Γ ⊢ t : Q↑` and `Γ ⊬ t : Q`; equivalently a type-level
`Q`-dependent conversion at `Prop` or `Type` between `q`-free types. Mechanisms to try:
self-supporting cycles (two casts along equations each available only through the other,
with `proofIrrel` on `q` as the base), large elimination of a `Prop` structure whose major
is `q`-free but typed only above, `structEta` on a `q`-free term typed only above, universe
polymorphic `Q` whose inhabitant exists only at some levels. For each either formalise it
(larger-context derivation in Lean; separating invariant for the smaller context) or prove
why it fails. Also attempt the positive partial result the regress argument offers: `Cancel`
for `Q : Sort u` with `u ≉ 0` and `Q` a rigid non-structure type (so `q` is never a proof,
a major or an eta subject).
