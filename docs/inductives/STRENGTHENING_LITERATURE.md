**My judgement is that `weakN_iff`, quantified over every WF environment, is probably false (about 85% confidence).** The larger-context derivation is convincing; the remaining obligation is a complete soundness and environment-formation argument for the countermodel.

Regarding the literature, **Carneiro explicitly states strengthening** in *The Type Theory of Lean* (2019), §3.2, “Regularity,” lemma “Weakening,” parts (3–4). Part (4) removes an extension when neither equality endpoint mentions it. The proof says “(1,2) and (3,4) are each proven by mutual induction on the first hypothesis,” without caveat. That does **not explain the transitivity case**: the intermediate term can mention the removed variable. His treatment of singleton elimination permits precisely proof fields and data fields appearing as indices; §4.1 also asserts that the proof-field projections needed for enhanced reduction can be defined using recursors. That assertion needs particular care for dependent indices. [Carneiro, *The Type Theory of Lean*, §3.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/digama0/lean-type-theory/v1.0/typesys.tex), [“Inductive types / Large elimination”](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/digama0/lean-type-theory/v1.0/axioms.tex), [§4.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/digama0/lean-type-theory/v1.0/unique.tex).

The later paper is more decisive about status: Carneiro, *Lean4Lean: Verifying a Typechecker for Lean, in Lean*, **§2.4, Conjecture 2.10**, explicitly labels strengthening conjectural. The same section retracts the thesis’s uniqueness/inversion proofs because their derivation stratification does not support the required substitution bound. That is a separately identified gap, not a published refutation of strengthening. [Paper, §2.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.14064).

Related results require distinguishing their calculi. Abel–Coquand–Pagano, *A Modular Type-checking Algorithm for Type Theory with Singleton Types and Proof Irrelevance*, §§2.2–2.3 and §§5–7, establish normalization/equality-checking results for a different proof-irrelevance mechanism, with restricted elimination; their singleton types are not Lean’s indexed singleton Props. Coquand–Spiwack explicitly report **failure of strengthening** in *A Proof of Strong Normalisation using Domain Theory* (LICS 2006), §4.4: their anonymous proof constant hides the variable witnessing inhabitation. This is a relevant precedent, but a different counterexample. [Abel–Coquand–Pagano](https://lmcs.episciences.org/1069/pdf); [Coquand–Spiwack, §4.4](https://assert-false.science/arnaud/papers/A%20proof%20of%20strong%20normalisation%20using%20domain%20theory%20%282006%29.pdf).

Werner’s *On the Strength of Proof-Irrelevant Type Theories*, §§2.4 and 3, supplies guarded equality elimination and structural metatheory, but I found no context-strengthening theorem there. His Conjecture 3.14 concerns **normalization**; Abel–Coquand refute it in *Failure of Normalization in Impredicative Type Theory with Proof-Irrelevant Propositional Equality*, section “Counterexample to Normalization.” Neither result establishes failure of strengthening for Lean’s singleton families. I found no published treatment of this exact counterexample. [Werner](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.3928); [Abel–Coquand](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.08174).

extends arbitrary dependent branches, with identity transport at constructors. At \(v=2\), the two origins differ, allowing empty versus terminal output while \(P\,2\) is false. **The riskiest rule is `elimIota`—equivalently, the native iota equations admitted through `extra`.** One must establish strict computation and substitution stability for the full dependent eliminator, uniformly over motives and universes. The finite transport calculations do not alone supply that theorem. Standard groupoid Π-semantics supports beta/eta; terminal proof fibers support proof irrelevance. But the impredicative Prop extension, universe coherence, conversion, and every installed equation still need a combined soundness proof. Hofmann–Streicher, *The Groupoid Interpretation of Type Theory*, §§4.6–4.8, 4.12, supplies the underlying machinery, not this extension.
